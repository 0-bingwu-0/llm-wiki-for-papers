# Set Me Up

Make this project mine through a short Q&A. Ask these one at a time, and wait for each answer before asking the next. Keep each question to a sentence or two, with a quick example if it helps.

1. **What's your research area?** The area and the questions it covers, such as "memory for LLM agents" or "video generation with diffusion models".
2. **What are you working on right now?** Your current project or question. The "For my work" part of every paper page is judged against it. If you don't have a project yet, a direction is fine.
3. **How should papers be explained to you?** How deep you are in the area, and what you look at first in a paper: the mechanism, the evaluation, the theory, the cost.
4. **Which papers do you have?** Drop PDFs into `library/`, or paste arXiv links or titles. A handful is enough to start; you can add more anytime.

If my setup message already answers some of these, skip them.

Then, in one go:

* Rewrite [profile.md](../context/profile.md) from my answers, in my own words, under its headings. Drop the example content. Keep it short and specific.
* Delete the example wiki: every page in [wiki/papers/](../wiki/papers/) and [wiki/topics/](../wiki/topics/). They were written for the example setup.
* Build the wiki from my papers, following "Many papers at once" in [add.md](add.md). This rewrites [index.md](../wiki/index.md) for my area.
* Tell me it's done in a few sentences: how many papers, which topics came out, and the one connection or disagreement that stands out most. Link the index. Then offer three ways to go on: add more papers, ask a question, or start on the reading list.
